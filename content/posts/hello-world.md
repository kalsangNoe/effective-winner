## Why start a blog

I have spent years telling myself I would write more. Notes piled up in scratch files, half-formed thoughts evaporated on walks, and the good ideas were never where I left them. A blog is a forcing function: a place with an audience of at least one.

The bar here is deliberately low. Publish something small, publish it often, and let the archive accumulate into something worth reading.

## What to expect

Mostly engineering notes, a few essays, and the occasional rabbit hole documented in embarrassing detail.

### Cadence

Roughly weekly, when it works. No promises, no streak counter guilting me into filler.

## Closing thought

Writing is thinking made visible. If you are reading this, thanks for thinking alongside me.
