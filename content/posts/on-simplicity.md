## The cost of cleverness

Every clever abstraction is a loan against your future understanding. The interest compounds quietly until one day a small change requires reading the entire codebase.

## Deep modules

The best interfaces are small doors into large rooms: a simple surface hiding substantial capability. Shallow modules invert that ratio and leak their complexity onto everyone who touches them.

### Information hiding

A module earns its keep by absorbing complexity so callers do not have to. If using it requires knowing how it works, the encapsulation has failed.

### Naming

A name that needs a comment to explain it is a name that has not finished being chosen. Precise names are the cheapest documentation you will ever write.

## Simplicity is earned

Simple is not the same as easy. It is the residue of many deletions, the shape left behind after you remove everything that was not essential.
